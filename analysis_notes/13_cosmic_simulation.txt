CANCER GENOMICS - COSMIC MUTATIONAL SIGNATURE SIMULATION
========================================================

Reference genome: GRCh37 / hg19
Genomic regions: chr12 exons from GENCODE hg19 annotation
Signature database: COSMIC v3.2
Signatures simulated: SBS1 and SBS7c
Inference software: MutationalPatterns 3.12.0

SIMULATION RESULTS
------------------
Experiment          Target SBS1    Generated SBS1    Inferred SBS1    Absolute error
100 SNVs, 70:30     70%            71.0%             66.53%           4.47 percentage points
500 SNVs, 70:30     70%            71.4%             72.22%           0.82 percentage points
1000 SNVs, 70:30    70%            71.0%             70.51%           0.49 percentage points
500 SNVs, 50:50     50%            49.0%             48.84%           0.16 percentage points

INTERPRETATION
--------------
The simulation evaluated the recovery of known COSMIC SBS1 and SBS7c
signature mixtures using different mutation counts and mixture ratios.

For the 70:30 experiments, inference error decreased as the number
of mutations increased from 100 to 1000.

The 500-mutation 50:50 mixture was also recovered accurately.

These experiments suggest that mutation count can influence
signature inference accuracy. However, only one random realization
was analyzed per condition, so the results are not sufficient to
establish a general statistical relationship.

The evaluation fitted only the two generating signatures.
Consequently, this is a controlled signature recovery experiment
rather than an assessment of performance against all COSMIC signatures.
