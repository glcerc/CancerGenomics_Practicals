BAM QUALITY CONTROL
===================

Objective:
Evaluate the alignment quality and sequencing coverage
of the HCC1143 normal sample using SAMtools.

Input:
HCC1143.normal.21.19M-20M.bam

Tools:
SAMtools 1.13, Bash, AWK

RESULTS
-------
QC-passed alignment records: 511,065
QC-failed alignment records: 61,666
QC-passed mapped reads: 99.01%
QC-passed properly paired reads: 97.55%
QC-passed duplicate rate: 4.78%
Covered positions: 997,763
Mean depth (covered positions): 47.60x
Maximum depth: 280x

INTERPRETATION
--------------
The sequencing data show high alignment and pairing
rates among QC-passed records.

The mean sequencing depth across covered positions
is 47.60x.

However, alignment statistics alone cannot establish
the reliability of individual variant calls.
Base quality, mapping quality, and variant-level
evidence should also be evaluated.
